=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-= Summary + Initial Mechanics ==-=-=-=-=-=-=-=-=
Clawguelike is a physics-based, single-player roguelike arcade game for Steam inspired by the mechanics of Raccooin: Coin Pusher Roguelike. The core gameplay loop revolves around a round-based structure where players manipulate a magnetic claw to harvest a chaotic pile of 3D items inside a machine, earning cash to spend in a strategic shop between rounds. Moving away from frantic, time-pressured arcade dashes, the game embraces a deliberate, cozy-yet-tactical puzzle pacing governed by a limited-pull system (starting at around 2 pulls per round, upgradeable via shop progression). Players retain full horizontal control on the X/Z axes to carefully position their shot, then trigger an automated descent, removing time stress and focusing attention entirely on precision, physics interactions, and clever upgrade synergies.
